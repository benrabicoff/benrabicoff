# Hi, I’m Ben 👋

I design and build digital products, with a focus on SaaS, complex workflows, and experiences that feel clear and effortless.

My work spans product strategy, interaction design, product systems, and frontend development. I’m most useful when a product is complicated, the requirements are still taking shape, and the path forward needs to become clearer.

## What I do

I work across the product development process, from defining the problem and shaping the product direction to designing the experience and helping bring it into production.

That often includes:

- Turning ambiguous ideas into clear product requirements
- Simplifying complex workflows and operational processes
- Designing interfaces, interaction patterns, and design systems
- Prototyping and testing product concepts
- Working closely with engineering through implementation
- Contributing directly to frontend development when useful

I care less about staying inside a specific discipline and more about doing whatever helps the product become clearer, more useful, and easier to build.

## How I work

I approach products as connected systems rather than collections of individual screens.

I want to understand the underlying rules, edge cases, dependencies, and user decisions before polishing the interface. Once the structure is sound, the visual design usually becomes simpler.

A few principles guide my work:

- Start with the real problem, not the requested feature
- Reduce complexity without hiding important information
- Design for the full workflow, including uncommon and difficult cases
- Make decisions that can survive implementation
- Prefer clear systems over one-off solutions
- Treat product, design, and engineering as overlapping responsibilities

## Current work

I’m currently building [Breely](https://breely.com), a scheduling and business management platform for service-based businesses.

My work includes product strategy, UX and interface design, frontend development, positioning, onboarding, and the operational details involved in growing a SaaS product.

Building the product directly has shaped how I think about design. Good decisions have to work not only for the user, but also for the business, the codebase, support, marketing, and everything that happens after a feature ships.

## Background

Previously, I worked at Squarespace and LifeLock.

My experience includes established product organizations, startups, acquisitions, and independently built products. That range has made me comfortable moving between strategic questions and detailed implementation work.
